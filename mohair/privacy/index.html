MOHAIR PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 29 October 2026Effective: 29 October 2026

THE SHORT VERSION

  • We collect only what Mohair needs to work: your sign-in details, the patterns you upload and your projects.
  • Your patterns are private to you. Our AI reads a pattern only when you ask it to.
  • We don't sell your data and there are no ads.
  • Your data is stored in the EU (Germany and the Netherlands).
  • You can delete your account and everything in it at any time.

4.HOW AI READS YOUR PATTERNS

  • Mohair uses AI to split a pattern into steps. This happens only after you tap to start it.
  • To do this, we send the pattern's text and page images to Anthropic, the company behind the Claude AI model. Anthropic processes them on our behalf under a data processing agreement and does not use them to train its models.
  • To catch mistakes, the founder of MB Random powers may look at parsed patterns and compare them with the original. Patterns are never shared with other users or published.
WE DON'T USE YOUR PATTERNS TO TRAIN AI MODELS.

6.SIGNING IN WITH APPLE OR GOOGLE

We ask Apple or Google only for your email and name. We use them to sign you in and to show your name in the app, nothing else.

Mohair's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7.WHERE YOUR DATA IS STORED

Your account and content are stored in the EU. When a pattern is read by AI, it is sent to Anthropic in the USA. Some of our providers are US companies even when their servers are in the EU. In each case the transfer is protected by the EU's Standard Contractual Clauses, and where the provider takes part in it, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

9.YOUR RIGHTS

You can ask us to:

  • show you the data we hold about you, or send you a copy;
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • stop or limit a use of it, including the pattern check in section 4;
  • withdraw a consent you gave.

Email hello@randompowers.com, or delete your account in the app. We reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm it's really you.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to Lithuania's State Data Protection Inspectorate (vdai.lrv.lt) or the data protection authority where you live.

10.CHILDREN

Mohair is not for children under 14. If we learn that a child under 14 has an account, we will delete it.

11.SECURITY

Data is encrypted between your device and our servers. Your patterns and files are only served to you after you sign in, and only our team can access our systems. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach affects you, we'll tell you as the law requires.

12.CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

When we change this policy we update the date at the top. If a change matters to you, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
